Facial proportion analysis · runs in your browser

Measured,
not judged.

Contour maps your facial proportions with an on-device model, scores them against common (and culture-bound) reference ranges, and turns the observations into a practical, lifestyle-only action plan — sleep, skin, hydration, habits, and styling.

  • Runs 100% in your browser
  • No upload · no account
  • Your photo never leaves the device

For curiosity and self-care — not a beauty verdict, diagnosis, or medical advice. Proportion “ideals” are cultural heuristics, not objective truth. Intended for adults.
